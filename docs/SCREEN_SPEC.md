# Screen Spec

Current layout and behavior of every screen. Items marked **[PLANNED]** are decided but not built (owned by tasks in `tasks/`). Viewport target 1366x900.

## Shared UI pieces (AO-030)
- **Tooltip (`Tip`, `src/ui/Tip.tsx`):** the only tooltip; no native `title` attribute is used anywhere. `<Tip tip={...}>child</Tip>` shows one `.tip` node in a portal at once (0 ms delay) on pointer hover or keyboard focus (elements that are not natively focusable get `tabIndex=0`), placed 8px from its target below or above (`placeTip`, tested) and always inside the viewport with an 8px margin, following the target, hidden on leave, blur, Esc, scroll, pointer down or when the target disappears. Content is `{title, icon, tag, body, lines[]}` built from data in `src/ui/tipContent.ts` (status effects with their amount and remaining turns, Block, relics with rarity and full text, role badges, Mana cost gem "Costs N Mana to play", Deck/Discard piles, Gold, Food with daily upkeep/production/net and days left, Threat with the enemy size multiplier, Day, Chapter, Boss day, building slots, building cost/effect, hero stats). Status names and effect texts come from the engine (`STATUS_INFO`, `statusEffectText(id, amount)`), never a UI copy. Relic tips show the benefit text in the normal colour and each of the relic's `drawbacks` as its own red line (`relicBenefit` strips the drawbacks from the description); the Mana cost tip of a cheaper "+" card reads "Costs 1 (was 2)." Every Mana gem (hand card, large card) carries the "Costs N Mana to play" tip and is not a separate tab stop (`tabIndex=-1`). After a popup hands focus back to its opener the tip stays quiet for 200 ms (`quietTipsBriefly`), so a closed modal never leaves the opener's tooltip open.
- **Modal (`src/ui/Modal.tsx`):** the one popup shell (unit, card, deck/pile, hero, food, pause, settings, city buildings, event pickers, city confirm, card removal). Portal, backdrop = the layer (click outside or the close button or Esc closes the top modal only), gold plaque header overlapping the top edge, stone / iron / wood panel, optional gold trim for info popups, focus moves into it, Tab is trapped, focus returns to the opener. A modal opened over another gets a darker backdrop. Its body is a `ScrollArea`. Omitting `onClose` makes a decision modal (Army full) that cannot be dismissed. While any modal is open Space does not end the turn.
- **Card info popup (`CardInfo.tsx`, AO-D040):** right-click any card (hand, reward, merchant, removal picker, event card picker, deck and pile viewers) calls `useCardInfo().open`. Gold-trim modal titled with the resolved card name ("Charge +" when upgraded, gold name on the card): the large card on the left, on the right type and rarity, source (unit skill / hero card / neutral), full text, Mana cost (with "was N" when the "+" is cheaper), Exhaust / Retain notes, for a hero-cast card (AO-D088) the line "Scales with <Stat>. <Stat> N: +40% spell damage." (N = the hero's current stat from `run.hero.stats`, handed to the provider by `useCardInfoHero`; the percent is `heroSpellScaling(N)`, never re-computed), and a well with the play condition (`cardRequirement`, warning icon) and, in battle, the current `cardPlayability` (green "Playable right now." or the red reason).
- **Unit and hero art (AO-040, DL-10):** every unit picture is a 32x32 pixel sprite drawn by `UnitArt` (`src/ui/UnitArt.tsx`), every hero picture a bust drawn by `HeroArt`; see DESIGN_LANGUAGE 7.6 for format, teams and scales. Player sprites are warm terracotta, enemy sprites are steel blue and mirrored to face the player; every enemy colour also leans 22% toward a cold blue (palette substitution in `sprite.ts`) and a one-pixel cold halo outlines the picture, so goblins, orcs, shamans and wolves read as the enemy team even where their palette has no team key. Battle tiles and army slots show them at 64px with a two-frame idle bob; Barracks cards, event unit offers, unit popup (96px), hero plaque (32px), hero popup (96px), hero setup (64px) and the stack previews (32px) use the same component. The 16x16 `unit_*` / `hero_*` icons no longer exist; the role badges remain icons.
- **Conditional cards (AO-D040):** a hand card whose condition or Mana is not met is dimmed. When the cause is not just Mana it also carries a small warning icon top-right; hovering shows a tip with the reason (`cardPlayability`) and the requirement, and clicking it toasts the reason instead of doing nothing. Large cards (reward, merchant, pickers) show the requirement as one small red line with a warning icon under the text (tooltip-only was too hidden where the player chooses what to add).
- **ScrollArea (`ScrollArea.tsx`, AO-043):** the one scrolling region (modal bodies, the City town, the Defeat / Victory statistics): the 16px pixel scrollbar plus a fade with a pulsing gold chevron on any edge that still has hidden content (`.scroll-cue`, pointer-events none, no pulse under reduced motion).
- **Keyboard and focus (AO-043):** hand cards, battle unit tiles and the Play Card zone are `role="button"` with `tabIndex=0`, an aria-label ("Swordsman, 12 units, not selectable", "Hold the Line, costs 1 Mana") and Enter / Space to act; the global focus ring (2px `--focus`, offset 2px) is the focus style. Every icon-only button (hero portrait, Deck, Discard, Log / History, Menu, drawer close) has an aria-label. **Space** ends the turn (or skips the running effect sequence) only when no popup is open and no control has keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`); a mouse-focused control does not count, so play by mouse keeps Space = End Turn. Esc closes the top modal, then the Battle Log / History drawer (focus returns to the button that opened it), and cancels a pending card or pick-up.
- **Load path and crash (AO-043):** the saved run is read with `readSavedRun` (`saveLoad.ts`): `validateSave(JSON.parse(raw))`; a missing, unparsable, damaged or newer-version save is "no save" (title screen, Continue disabled, "No saved run yet."). `ErrorBoundary` (main.tsx, around the game) shows a stone panel "Something went wrong" with the error message, **Back to menu** (remounts the game, the save is kept) and **Clear save and restart** (deletes `aod_run_state_v1` first).
- **Deck viewer (`DeckViewer.tsx`):** read-only modal of hand-size cards in a well, grouped with a `xN` chip, sorted by Mana cost then name (never draw order), upgraded copies listed apart with a `+` name, tabs with counts (`Tab` plates). Right-click a card for its info popup (stacked).

## Card upgrades in the UI (AO-D060, AO-035)
A "+" card whose effective cost is lower than the base card shows its cost gem in moss green (`.cost-cheaper`, on the hand card and on large cards) with the "was N" tip; an unaffordable card keeps the grey gem. `cardView` carries `baseManaCost` for this.
`src/ui/cardView.ts` (`cardView(cardId, upgraded)`, tested) is the one place the UI asks "what does this card show and cost": it reads `resolveCard` (name "Charge +", effective Mana cost) and `CARD_UPGRADES[id].description` for the text. Hand cards, large cards, the card info popup, the deck viewer (grouped and sorted by the effective cost, "+" copies apart) and the card in flight all use it; `handleCardClick`, the hand's affordability/condition marks and `cardPlayability(cardId, state, upgraded)` use the instance's `upgraded` flag, so an upgraded card is played (and refused) at its real cost. A "+" card has a gold name.

## Title
`TitleScreen` (`data-screen="title"`): candle-glow vignette, a code-drawn pixel castle skyline along the bottom (`TitleSkyline`, one SVG of stepped rects in three flat layers, no image files), the crest, the gold ribbon plaque "Ashes of Dominion" (Display 44 on a 72px ribbon, crest 48px: AO-043 shrank the logo so logo, skyline and menu slab balance at 1366x900 and 1280x720), the tagline and a stone slab (360 wide) holding **New Run** (primary), **Continue** and **Settings**. Without a saved run Continue is disabled and the line "No saved run yet." sits under it. **New Run** while a run is in progress (any phase except Defeat / Run complete) opens "Abandon your current run?" (Cancel / Confirm) before the hero screen.

## Hero + relic (one screen, AO-D029)
`HeroSetupScreen` replaces the two setup steps. Top: title and the commander name input (Start stays disabled while the name is empty or whitespace, with a hint beside the buttons). Left column: three hero cards (portrait, class, tagline, five stat rows; each row's Tip comes from the `HERO_STAT_INFO` map in `tipContent.ts`: what the stat does in this game with the engine's numbers, plus "Now: ..." for the value; Intelligence and Vitality say "Not used yet". AO-D064 will widen Strength / Dexterity / Intelligence: edit that one map) and the five starting relics from `startingRelicList()` as two-column choice cards (rarity-framed icon, name, benefit text with each drawback as a red line; hover shows the relic tip). Below 800px of height (1280x720) the relics become three columns of 48px-icon cards and the setup paddings shrink, so all five relics show without scrolling. The first hero and relic are preselected; the selected card carries the gold triple line. Right column, sticky (`.hero-setup-side`): the preview from `previewStart(heroId, relicId)` (army in Front / Back rows with unit art, counts and names, then Units, Max Mana, Gold, Food and the relic's `effectSummary` lines), so picking a hero or relic changes it at once (Traveler's Purse: Gold 150, Royal Banner: a larger stack). Under the preview, in the same sticky column so it is visible at 1366x900 and 1280x720 without scrolling, sit **Back** (returns to the title) and one **Start** button that calls `createRun(seed, heroId, name, relicId)` and lands on the Road.

## Road (world map) — meta family
- **Scene (top, fills remaining height):** `Chapter n — Step x / y` (a wood chip, gold Silkscreen 16, uppercase), the "You are here — <type>" pill (the `start` node reads "Start"), an always-available **Enter City** button beside it, a red **food alert** strip when `foodWarning` (starving forecast with expected deaths and the morale malus, or days of Food left), "Choose Your Path" and the path cards. When the boss window opens (`bossWarning`, last 7 days) a red banner "The boss draws near: N days left." shows once per chapter per session (click or 6 s to dismiss); while the boss window is open the scene reserves 64px of top padding for it, so nothing hides behind the banner and nothing moves when it goes.
- **Path cards:** 250w x 300h plaques for battle / fort / mine / merchant / event / village / boss (AO-D076, AO-D077), node-type accent border, corner badge icon (`node_fort`: palisade camp with a red banner; `node_mine`: timbered mine entrance with gold ore). Village, fort and mine cards (and their Scouted icons) carry a tip: village both choices (Raid: Gold and Food, Threat +1; Help: a smaller gift, then Food a day and militia a week from `VILLAGE`), fort "Assault an enemy camp: a hard battle, then a relic and extra loot" (its relics never carry a drawback), mine "Capture it: a small find now" plus "+1 Gold every day (at most 6 mines pay)" from `MINE`. At 640px of scene height or less (1280x720 leaves 520px) a container query lets the row take the space the banner, alert strip and Scouted strip leave: cards shrink from 300h (badge 96px, tighter gaps) and are never clipped by the bottom bar (measured at 1280x720, 1366x768, 1366x900, 1600x900 with alert strip, banner and scouted strip). A node shows when its visibility is not `unknown` or its layer is within `worldMap.revealedUntilStep`; layers beyond the next one that were scouted appear as a small "Scouted ahead" icon strip under the cards.
- **Enter City:** opens a confirm popup. While `nextCityVisitRaisesThreat(run)` it shows `CITY_VISIT_WARNING`, the current enemy strength (`enemyStrengthAfterCityVisits(run)`, x1.00 at Threat 0) and the strength after one more visit; when the visit is free (AO-D070: the first of the run and of every chapter) it shows a green "No Threat increase" line and the unchanged strength. Stay on the Road / Enter City (`TRAVEL_TO_CITY`, no days, no Food). Leaving the city returns to the map and toasts "Enemies grew stronger: Threat N (xM enemy strength)".
- **Bottom bar (200px, shared with City; one component, `GarrisonBar`, takes the run):** 3px top border, padding 8px 16px, 181px content height, 8px spacing scale (4px only between relic cells), column gap 16. Four columns (five in the City, AO-D086):
  (Floaters) Resource floaters: when Gold or Food differ from what the bar showed the last time it was on screen (a day of travel, battle loot, mine captures, events, starvation, purchases; `resourceDeltas`) a floating **+N** (moss) / **-N** (blood) with the resource icon rises above the Gold / Food pill for 1.8 s. One signed number per resource; no toast repeats it (the loot / resource toasts were removed, the reward screen has the loot pills).
  1. Resources (272 content + 16 padding + 4px divider), four 32px rows with 8px gaps; pills use 8px side padding and size by content (`min-width: max-content`) so `Day 99`, five-digit Gold or `123  -2/day` plus the starvation icon never reach the stepped clip: **Gold | Threat** (AO-D084: Gold is a button that shows the stockpile and the daily net, e.g. `500  +12/day`, from `dailyChange`; click opens the **Gold popup** built like the Food popup: stockpile, Gold Mine building, captured mines (at most `MINE.payingMines` pay), daily costs (none), net); **Food** (stockpile and daily net, e.g. `50  -2/day`, red with a warning glyph when `foodWarning`; click opens the Food popup with per-stack upkeep, Farm production, net, days left, starvation forecast); **Day | Ch. n/3**; **Boss in N days** (turns red and pulses in 1s pixel steps inside the warning window; static under reduced motion).
  2. Hero (176 content + 16 + 2px divider, AO-D042): plaque 24h, portrait 40h (a button: click opens the **Hero popup**), 4px gaps, 3x5 relic grid of 32x32 cells with 4px gaps (hover = relic tip with rarity and full text; empty = dashed).
  2b. Garrison (City only, AO-D086; 168 content + 16 + 4px divider, between Hero and Army): an iron plaque "Garrison" and a 2x2 grid of 72x72 slots, one per Barracks tier (`garrisonSlots`): a filled slot shows the unit sprite (x1) and `waiting/cap` (`garrisonRows`, cap from `garrisonCap`); a click collects that unit type (`COLLECT_GARRISON` with the unit id), disabled with a Tip reason (`collectBlocker`: nobody waiting, or army full) when it cannot; slots of tiers not yet built are dashed and empty. This is the only place to collect the garrison.
  3. Army (flexible zone, block centered): the 3x2 grid in true board positions. Top row = front, positions 1-3; bottom row = back, positions 4-6; columns = left / center / right lane. A 16px gutter left of the grid carries the rotated FRONT (accent) and BACK (dim) row labels. Slots are 86h, 240w (shrink to a 168 minimum on narrow viewports), 8px gaps. Filled slot: 64x64 icon with a 20px role badge, count `×N` and unit name; empty slot: dashed "Empty".
  4. Buttons (64 wide): **Deck** (opens the deck viewer: every owned card, tabs All / Attack / Defense / Buff / Debuff / Utility, in the City with a **Remove a card** button that opens the Town Hall where removal lives), Log (history drawer) and Menu, each 64x48.
- **Reposition (AO-D015/D061):** drag a filled slot (pointer events, mouse and touch; drag starts after 6px, an 80% ghost of the tile follows the pointer) or left-click it and then click a slot. The target decides the result: empty slot = `MOVE_STACK`, different unit type = swap (`MOVE_STACK`), same unit type = merge (`MERGE_STACKS`, the result stays on the target slot, green frame, "Merge" tag while hovering, a "Merged" flourish inside the slot afterwards). While holding, every other slot pulses; a drop anywhere else, Esc, or a click outside the grid cancels and nothing changes. Tooltips are off while a stack is held or dragged. The "Merged" flourish lasts 500ms. Dragging is off while any modal, popup or the History drawer is open. Right-click a stack opens the unit popup (never opened by dragging): stats, food use per day (`stackUpkeep`), **Split Off** and Merge, and **Dismiss** (count input + two-step confirm; whole stack allowed unless it is the last unit type, then at most count-1) calling `DISMISS_STACK`, outside battle only. **Split Off** asks for the count, closes the popup and holds the split-off part as a ghost (source slot shows the remaining count, empty slots pulse and read "Place here"); clicking an empty slot applies `SPLIT_STACK` then `MOVE_STACK` in one commit; clicking a stack of the same unit type (not the one the part came from; green frame, "Merge" tag on hover) applies `SPLIT_STACK` then `MERGE_STACKS` in one commit and does nothing at all if the merge is refused; a hint "Click an empty slot or a <unit> stack to merge. Esc cancels." floats above the bar; Esc or a click outside cancels and the split is never applied. With 6 stacks Split Off is disabled with a Tip. **Merge** in the popup closes it.
- Toasts (7 s for event outcome texts; they start under the floating Menu button, sit under the modal backdrop so they never cover an open city panel, and are cleared when the Defeat or Victory screen appears) and the History drawer (Esc closes it and focus returns to its button) use `runEventText.ts`: STARVED ("3 Swordsmen, 1 Archer starved"), MINE_CAPTURED ("Mine captured: +G Gold, +F Food, N mines"), BATTLE_LOOT, CHAPTER_STARTED, BOSS_DEFEATED, CITY_VISITED, THREAT_CHANGED, UNITS_GAINED/LOST/DISMISSED, UNIT_GAIN_DECLINED, FARM_UPGRADED, MAGE_TOWER_UPGRADED, DAILY_INCOME, EVENT_RESOLVED text.
- No floating corner menu button, no "New Run" (the pause menu has Main Menu).

## City — meta family (AO-038, AO-D062)
A Heroes 3 style town (`CityScreen.tsx`, panels and pure helpers in `src/ui/city/`; `cityView.ts` is tested). Warm stone and wood, `data-screen="city"`, the shared bottom bar below.
- **Header (sky):** the code-drawn castle skyline (`TitleSkyline fit="contain"`, 3px cells) on the horizon at the foot of a 120px sky band; at 640px of town height or less (1280x720, 1366x768) a container query slims the sky to 56px and tightens gaps and plot signs so all 12 plot cells show without scrolling, taller viewports keep 120px; over it the wood plaque with the city name (Ironhold) on the left and pills **Level N**, **Slots used/max** (e.g. 4/5) and **Gold** on the right. Under it a wood strip with the Threat notice, read from the `CITY_VISITED` event that brought the player here: a free visit says in green "No Threat increase: the first visit of the chapter is free" plus the current Threat and multiplier; a later visit says each visit makes enemies stronger (Threat +1) with the current Threat and the enemy size multiplier (`threatMultiplier`). Leave stays in the shared bar (red **Leave** under the resources) and is not duplicated in the header.
- **Plots (4 columns x 3 rows, left of the effects panel):** the top row is the four fixed hotspots, always open and taking no building slot: Town Hall, Barracks (AO-D080: always a built plot, status `N waiting` when the garrison holds soldiers, else `Recruit`), Temple and Marketplace; the eight optional buildings follow in scene order (Market, Stable, Forge, Training Hall, Farm, Gold Mine, Mage Tower, Shrine). A plot is a wood-framed window with the `bld_*` icon at x4 on a stone plinth and a wood sign (name, status). Level plaque (AO-D087, no check badge): a built plot shows `Lv N` in the corner of its art, `Max` at the top level (`buildingLevel` / `levelLabel`: Town Hall = city level, Barracks / Farm / Mage Tower = tier; every other building has a single level and so always reads `Max`). Buildings with several levels change their art (`buildingArt`, `BUILDING_LEVEL_ICONS`, levels spread evenly over the stages): Farm `bld_farm` patch -> `_2` barn and fields -> `_3` silo, barn and full fields; Barracks tent -> palisade hall with banner -> stone keep with two towers; Mage Tower plain -> orb-topped -> twin-turret with crystal. States (`plotState`): **built** (full colour, gold bevel, status Built, `+N Food/day` for the Farm, `Mana +N` for the Mage Tower, `N/M slots` for the Town Hall), **buildable** (dashed gold outline, muted icon, status "Build Ng"), **unaffordable** (dashed grey outline, red status "Need Ng"), **locked** (no free slot: greyed, no-entry badge, "No free slot"). Every plot opens its panel on click whatever its state; the shared `Tip` shows the effect, the cost and, when it cannot be built, the reason.
- **Active effects panel (right, 320px):** `activeEffects(run)` lists what the built buildings and the doctrine grant now, each with its pixel icon: Market discount, Stable (with the Food it saves the current army), Forge attack, Training Hall Mana, Farm Food per day (tier), Gold Mine Gold per day, Mage Tower Mana (tier), Shrine revival share, then the doctrine. Numbers come from engine exports where they exist (`FARM_TIERS`, `MAGE_TOWER_TIERS`, `GOLD_MINE_DAILY_GOLD`, `SHRINE_REVIVE_RATIO`, `dailyProduction`, `moveFoodCost`, the Forge `combatEffects`), otherwise from the engine's own description text. A footer "Per day" shows the city's Gold income and the daily Food net (`dailyFoodNet`); the two numbers are buttons that open the Gold popup and the Food popup (AO-D084). An empty list says so.
- **Building panels** (shared `Modal`, wood, wide, closed by Esc / backdrop / X; they stay open after a Build or Upgrade so the new state shows at once; an engine rejection still toasts):
  - **Barracks (920):** a strip with Gold and Food (20px, sticky: it stays at the top of the scrolling panel), army stacks (n of `MAX_ARMY_STACKS`), units and daily upkeep. Under it the **tier upgrade** (AO-D086, visible on both tabs): "Tier N of 4", "Next: Tier M, recruit <unit>, +W a week", the step cost (red when short) and **Upgrade to Tier M** (`UPGRADE_BARRACKS`, disabled with the Gold reason), or "Max tier". Then two tabs (`.tabs`): **Recruit** (default; the unit cards) and **Tiers** (the garrison note "grows every 7 days, next arrival in N days (day D), holds at most 2 weeks of each type" from `daysToGarrison` over the engine's `isGarrisonDay`, and the ladder I-IV from `BARRACKS_TIERS`: tier gem, unit pixel art, unit name, "Recruit, +N a week", the step cost on the next tier, Built / Next / Locked tag). Collecting the garrison is not done here: it lives in the bottom bar. The Recruit tab holds a note, then four unit cards, greyed with "Unlocks at tier N" (and the engine's `recruitBlocker` reason in a tip) for units above the current tier, live for the unlocked ones (pixel art, role badge with tip, name, description, HP / Damage / Attack / Defense, cost per unit in Gold and Food from `recruitCost` at 100 so the Market discount reads 6.8 not 7, Food upkeep per day), a `QuantityStepper` (small 32px **-** button, the number in a 20px display, **+**; the number is never typed; holding a button repeats, starting at 400ms and speeding up to 50ms; + stops at what Gold and Food afford; a **Max** button next to the total sets the largest affordable count), the total cost in Gold and Food (red when short), one line for where the recruits go (`addUnitsToArmy`: joins the stack of the type, or takes slot N; with 6 stacks and none of the type the card says the army is full and Recruit is disabled) and **Recruit N**. A line under the cards confirms the last recruit; the count resets to 1 after a recruit. Disabled reasons (Gold, Food, army full, tier) come from the engine's `recruitBlocker` through `recruitQuote`.
  - **Marketplace (620):** always available. A Gold / Food strip, a food-pack sketch, the price of one pack now and the next one (`foodMarketQuote`), the note "price rises 8% with every pack you buy and never falls back" plus the packs bought so far, a `QuantityStepper` (1 to what Gold affords, capped at `FOOD_MARKET.maxPacksPerAction`), **Max**, the total in Gold and Food and **Buy N packs** (`BUY_FOOD`, disabled with "Not enough Gold (N needed)").
  - **Town Hall (880):** left, the level ladder (I-III: slots per level, cost, Reached / Next / Locked) and **Upgrade to Level N**; right, card removal: the deck size and floor, the next four prices from `cityRemovalPrice` (Free, 50, 100, 200 ...), the uses so far and the **Remove a card** button (price or disabled reason from `cardRemovalQuote`) that opens the deck picker.
  - **Temple (880):** the four doctrines as large cards (icon, name, plain effect text, **Choose**). Choosing opens a stacked confirm ("This cannot be undone", Cancel / Confirm) before `CHOOSE_DOCTRINE`; once chosen the card shows Chosen and the others are dimmed "Not chosen".
  - **Mage Tower (I-III) and Farm (I-V) (720):** current tier and bonus, the one-slot note, the tier ladder (cumulative bonus, cost, Built / Next / Locked from `tierRows`) and one action: unbuilt = **Build Tier I** with the cost and the slot / Gold reason, built = **Upgrade to Tier N** (`UPGRADE_MAGE_TOWER` / `UPGRADE_FARM`) or "Fully upgraded". The Farm also shows production, upkeep and the net.
  - **Market, Forge, Stable, Shrine, Gold Mine, Training Hall (640):** icon, category tag, effect text (the live effect once built), slots used, cost and **Build** (with the reason under it), or the Built tag.
- **Bottom bar:** the same `GarrisonBar` as the Road plus the Garrison block; the resources column shows **Gold | Slots**, the Gold and Food pills (same popups) and **Leave**. The Deck viewer's **Remove a card** opens the Town Hall panel.
- Recruiting shows a "+N" flourish inside the recruited stack's slot. Reposition and the unit popup (with Dismiss) work here too.

## Merchant — overlay family
Top bar (title, gold pill), a shelf of 3 card offers + optional relic offer (rarity frame: common grey, rare steel, epic purple, rarity word on top, price under it), unaffordable = dimmed with a red price, ribbon **Leave** at the bottom-left. Below the shelf a **Remove a card (price)** button opens the deck picker (price rises per use; disabled reasons shown). A toast confirms the removal; the screen stays open.

## Event — overlay family
Rendered from `eventView(run)`: centered `?` badge, plaque title, description, option cards (label + effect text). An option with `available: false` is dimmed and shows its `reason` in red and does not react to clicks. A click calls `CHOOSE_EVENT_OPTION`; the outcome text (`EVENT_RESOLVED.text`) is a long toast and a History line. Sub-steps:
- **Card picker** (`choice.kind = 'card'`): modal grid of the eligible cards, identical copies grouped into one card with an `xN` chip (a click acts on the first copy; the engine already lists only `isUpgradable` instances for an upgrade), scrolling inside the modal so the last row is always reachable, titled "Choose a card to upgrade / remove / give away", close button = `CANCEL_EVENT_CHOICE`, a click = `CHOOSE_EVENT_CARD`. For an upgrade every card is shown as its "+" version with the gold "Upgrade" tag and a "Now: ..." line with the current text.
- **Unit picker** (`choice.kind = 'unit'`, Mercenary Camp): two unit tiles, click = `CHOOSE_EVENT_UNIT`, Cancel = `CANCEL_EVENT_CHOICE`.
- **Army full** (`run.pendingUnitChoice`): modal "Your army is full" with the outcome text and all army stacks plus the newcomer (tagged New, gold ring) as tiles showing count and food per day; a click opens the unit popup where Dismiss (whole or part, the newcomer included) calls `DISMISS_STACK`; **Turn the <unit> away** calls `DECLINE_UNIT_GAIN`. The event closes when the engine settles it.
- An ambush outcome hands the run to the battle screen, then the usual reward.

## Village — overlay family (AO-D072)
`VillageScreen.tsx`, `data-screen="village"`, phase `village` (`run.pendingVillage` = `villageOffer(chapter)`, fixed on arrival). A dusk sky over a code-drawn pixel village (`VillageScene.tsx`: hills, five cottages with lit windows and chimney smoke, pines; flat layers, no image files), the Village ribbon and Gold / Food pills at the top, a lead line (with the number of villages helped so far) and two wood panels. **Raid**: `+G Gold`, `+F Food` pills, a red "Threat +1 (now N, then N+1)" line with a Threat tip, red **Raid the village** (`RAID_VILLAGE`). **Help**: the smaller gift pills, the permanent effects in green ("+N Food per day, for the rest of the run", "+N militia per week in the city garrison (only the first M villages send any)", or "No more militia" once the cap is reached; from `VILLAGE`, `villageDailyFood`, `villageMilitiaPerWeek`) and gold **Help the village** (`HELP_VILLAGE`). A choice returns to the Road at once; a toast and a History line report the result (`VILLAGE_RAIDED` / `VILLAGE_HELPED`); Food per day then shows in the Food popup ("Villages: N helped") and the Food tip.

## Reward — overlay family
Banner "Victory! Choose One" ("Fort Taken! Choose One" after a fort, "The Boss Has Fallen!" after a boss), a loot line (pills "+N Gold", "+N Food" from the last `BATTLE_LOOT`), an optional relic block, up to 3 large cards (cost medallion, icon, name, description). AO-D068: there is no Skip and no Remove a card here (removal stays in the Merchant and the City); the player must pick a card or the upgrade slot.
- **Relic block:** a fort victory has already granted its relic (`relicGained`, `RELIC_CLAIMED`): a "Relic gained" heading and one read-only relic tile (no hover lift, no click) above the choices; when the fort's simple relic pool is used up (`relicGained` null) the heading reads "The camp held no relic" and the loot pills are the bonus. A boss victory shows `relicChoices` ("Boss spoils: take one relic", 0-3 cards; an empty list shows no relic row). Each is a relic card (`RelicOfferCard`, also used by the Merchant; on the Reward screen it is the slim `compact` tile: 64px icon on the left, rarity, name and effect on the right, so relic row, cards and buttons fit 1280x720) with a rarity frame (common grey, rare steel, epic purple), rarity word, benefit text plus a red drawback line, the relic tooltip on hover and a relic info popup on right-click (icon, rarity, text); a click on a boss relic calls `CLAIM_RELIC`, the row disappears and the screen stays open for the normal pick. Under the boss relic row a red line states "Picking a card forfeits the unclaimed relics."
- **Upgrade slot:** a not-yet-upgraded deck card from `upgradeOptions` appears as the base card previewed as its "+" version (`resolveCard(cardId, true)`: "Charge +", the "+" text and cost) with the gold "Upgrade" tag and a line "Now (N Mana): <base text>"; a click calls `CLAIM_UPGRADE`.
- Card pick: one pick only, no Confirm: clicking a card (or upgrade) applies it and the screen closes.

## Deck picker (shared)
Modal (plaque "Remove a card - price") listing the whole master deck as large cards, identical copies grouped with an `xN` chip; clicking one removes one copy of it and closes the picker, right-click shows its info. Used by Merchant and City. Modals render in a portal so clipped popups cannot trap them.

## Hero popup (AO-D040)
Click the hero portrait (Road/City bar, battle hero chip). Gold-trim modal titled with the hero name: portrait and class on the left; on the right a well with one row per stat (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Vitality, Wisdom: icon, value, what it does now, derived from the engine formulas; each row is a Tip with the `HERO_STAT_INFO` text) plus Mana (max), the relic list as 48px rarity frames each with its relic tip, and a dashed **Level / XP - Coming soon** block (no XP mechanic exists yet, AO-D030; the hero has `level`/`xp` fields that nothing changes). Intelligence and Vitality say "Not used yet" because the engine does not read them (AO-D064 will change that for Intelligence, Dexterity and Strength).

## Defeat / Run complete (AO-D027, AO-031)
`RunEndScreen` (`data-screen` defeat or victory), one component that fills the viewport in three parts so the buttons never scroll away (AO-043): a fixed header (ribbon plaque, blood for Defeat; the cause line from `runSummary(run).causeLabel`; hero name and class), a `ScrollArea` with the statistics, and a fixed footer bar with **Main Menu** (back to the title, save kept) and **New Run** (clears `aod_run_state_v1`, disables Continue and returns to the title). The statistics are one centred 720px column of vertical lists, never side by side (`runEndView.ts`, tested):
- **Highlights:** three gold-framed tiles: Days survived, Largest stack, Enemies killed (the engine keeps no "biggest hit" stat yet, so it is not shown).
- **Ratio bars:** Damage dealt vs taken and Enemies killed vs units lost, one two-colour bar each (gold / blood share, values under it; `ratioShare`).
- **Grouped lists,** each a well of `.row`s with a pixel icon, the label and the value: **Battle** (battles won, forts taken, bosses, enemies killed, damage dealt / taken, turns, cards played), **Journey** (chapter reached, days, events, Threat), **Economy** (gold and food gathered, food eaten, relics collected), **Army** (units in the army now, largest stack, units recruited, lost, starved, revived). A row the engine does not send is omitted.
- **Strips:** the final army (unit art, count, name), the relics collected (rarity frames with the relic tip) and the hero (the five stats with their `HERO_STAT_INFO` tips plus max Mana) as a list.
No floating menu button on this screen; toasts are cleared when it appears.

## Battle — battle family
- **Frame:** full-screen ornate frame (pixel dragon ornaments). Top: raised iron plaques, hero (Display-face name, relics, Mana bar) on the left, turn (turn number, phase, hero HP) on the right. No floating menu button.
- **Body:** left rail = your 2x3 portrait grid (back column, front column), center = scene panel (empty: no narration text, AO-D023; holds the Play Card zone for no-target cards, the "All enemies fallen" banner (AO-D067) and the played card of AO-D011; the effects layer draws over the whole field, see Battle effects), right rail = enemy 2x3 grid (front column, back column). **End Turn** is the compact 150px button in the hand tray (AO-D063; the wide bar of AO-D024 is gone). The field (`.frame-body`) is a size container: tiles are 112px tall when the field allows it, otherwise a third of the field's height (three rows, two 8px gaps) with a 100px floor, so no tile is ever covered by the tray. Measured with `elementFromPoint` at every tile centre of a full 3x2 vs 3x2 boss board: 1280x720 (tiles 107px), 1366x768, 1366x900, 1600x900.
- **Portrait slot (96x112):** iron frame with a 4px team line (steel = yours, blood = enemy), 88x76 sprite window, role badge top-left, the count chip `xN` in the top-right corner of the sprite window (12px Silkscreen on a dark chip: the corner is empty in every sprite, so the count never covers the art), name row under the window. No HP strip, no block strip, no lock icon (AO-D024). Block and statuses are 16px icons with their amount stacked under the role badge; several applications of one status (three armor buffs) are one chip with the summed amount and a gold `xN` stack count (`groupStatuses`), and its Tip says "N applications added together". Selected = white ring + gold corner pixels; selectable = gold ring; dimmed when not relevant. State visuals (`stackStates` in `stackStatus.ts`, single UI mirror of the engine gate): **acted** (your stack, already used its action) = whole tile faded, not selectable; **frozen** (engine `cannotAct`: Freeze, or `cannotAttack`) = ice tint, jagged frost rim and shards, not selectable; a frozen enemy shows the floater "Skips turn" when the enemy playback starts (it has no step, AO-D066); **chained** (`cannotAttack` or `cannotMove`) = two crossing iron chains over the window, only `cannotAttack` makes it unselectable (a rooted stack can still attack); **blocked** (`isBlockedByFrontAlly`, AO-D069: a back-row MELEE stack while ANY friendly stack lives in the front row; ranged and support/healer stacks are never blocked) = small red no-entry `ui_blocked` marker bottom-right of the sprite window, not selectable, no overlay; its Tip reads "Front row is occupied: this melee unit cannot attack"; **fallen** (a stack at count 0) = a sunken window with the pixel grave `ui_grave`, a dashed blood rim and the name "Fallen", shown the moment the stack dies (the death fade plays over it) and never targetable as an enemy or heal target. Floating text shows `-N units` for kills and `Wounded` when the hit killed nothing; the battle log line carries HP damage, units killed and units left.
- **Bottom bar (hand tray, AO-D063):** Deck pile icon at far left (the count is in its tooltip and viewer), the hand in a row, End Turn (150px; "Finish Battle", 196px, once every enemy is dead) top-right, the Discard pile icon and the Log / Menu buttons at the far right. The tray has a fixed height (224px hand area), so the field never changes size when cards are played or drawn or during the enemy playback (measured at hand sizes 0, 3, 5, 8 and through a played card plus a whole enemy turn: the field, tray and top bar rectangles never change). With more cards than fit, the slots narrow and the cards overlap like a fan (the last card is always whole, hover lifts one); the row never scrolls. The draw pile is drawn as a stack. The piles are buttons: clicking either opens the pile viewer (deck viewer with tabs Draw / Discard, and Exhausted when it has cards; headers "Draw pile - N", "Discard pile - N"; contents grouped and sorted by cost with the order hidden). The hero chip portrait opens the Hero popup. Draw = cards slide in from the deck; end of turn = leftover cards slide to the discard pile.
- **Turn effects strip (AO-043):** in the middle of the top bar (two chip rows at most, so the bar never grows): pixel chips for the buffs the army carries right now, read from the stacks' flags and statuses (`turnEffects`, tested): "Next friendly attack +50%" (Focus Fire, read from `combat.nextFriendlyAttackBonusPercent`; hero spells do not consume it), "Next attack +60%" (a per-stack Focus Shot style bonus, naming the stack), "Damage taken -N%", "Counter N%", "Protect N%", "Divine shield", "Evasion", "Untargetable", and one chip per strength / armor / taunt status with the summed amount ("Armor +6" after Hold the Line). Each chip has a Tip with the detail, the stacks it applies to and when it ends ("used up when the stack attacks", "Lasts until the start of your next turn", "Lasts N more turns"); the strip is empty (absent) when nothing is active and chips disappear with the effect.
- **Interaction:** click your stack to start its free action, then click a legal enemy (Priest: any friendly stack). Card flow (AO-D064): a card is chosen by click (or by starting a drag). **Hero-cast / `enemy-stack` cards** (Fireball, Frost, Chain Lightning, Command: Strike, Volley, Mark Target) need no acting stack: click an enemy (the valid targets are gold-ringed) or drop the card on it; your own units are never asked for. **`none` cards** (Arcane Storm, Arrow Rain, Focus Fire, Formation, Rally, Battle Hardened, Hold the Line, Mass Charge, Venomous Army...) play by clicking any empty part of the battlefield or by dropping the card on it (the field gets a dashed gold outline and the Play Card zone shows); an empty click therefore plays them instead of cancelling. Unit-bound and `ally-stack` cards keep the acting-unit selection, then their target; a card dropped on a unit acts like a click on it. Hand cards are HTML5-draggable while playable. Re-click a selected stack to deselect; `Esc` cancels; a click on bare battlefield (not a unit or the Play Card zone) deselects the stack and cancels the pending card, except that it plays a pending `none` card; `Space` ends the turn when nothing is pending, no popup or the log is open, you are not typing and no control has keyboard focus (see Keyboard and focus). Keyboard play: Tab to a unit tile or a card, Enter / Space to select or play it, Tab to a target or the Play Card zone and Enter to confirm. While an effect sequence runs (a played action, the enemy turn) a click anywhere or `Space` skips to its end and lands on the engine's final state; input is locked until then. Dead enemy stacks never act in playback.
- **No pos badge, no HP numbers** on portraits; the count is the health readout. Block and statuses appear as small icons with their amount on the portrait image (top-left), not as text under the name.
- **Right-click** any stack (yours or enemy) opens the unit info popup without split/merge, plus morale, veterancy, block and active statuses (each with its effect tip). Right-click a hand card opens the card info popup with the live playability reason.
- **Floating combat text** rises over the affected portrait for your actions and the enemy's alike: `-N units` (20px, on a dark plate with a blood edge) / `Wounded` / `No damage` (AO-D022), `Dodged`, `Blocked N`, `+N Block`, heal `+N units` (the soldiers actually restored), and a status name with its icon. Every hit cue of both sides has a floater on its target (tested against real engine turns). Floaters of one stack are queued at least 450ms apart and sit on three height lanes (`staggerFloaters`), so a multi-hit enemy turn reads as a list instead of one smeared line; each lives 1.6s. Reduced motion keeps the floaters and drops every effect.
- **Cannot act** (acted, frozen, cannotAttack, blocked back-row melee): the state visuals above, not selectable; no warning text.
- **Hero-cast effects (AO-D064):** a hit whose `attackerStackId` is `hero` looks up no stack. The effect leaves the hero portrait in the top plaque and travels to every target: an orb for Intelligence cards (Fireball, Frost, Chain Lightning, Arcane Storm), an arrow bolt for Dexterity (Arrow Rain, Volley), a fast bolt that lands as a slash for Strength (Command: Strike); the hit floaters (`-N units`, `Wounded`) land on the targets and never on the hero. The battle log reads "<Hero> casts <Card>." then "<Hero> hits <Unit> for N dmg...". Cards that scale (`card.scalesWith`) carry no badge on the hand tile or large card (AO-D088); the card info popup says which stat they scale with and what it adds.
- **Battle-end window (AO-D067):** when `combat.enemiesCleared` the End Turn button reads "Finish Battle" and a banner "All enemies fallen - heal up, then finish the battle" sits at the top of the field. Cards and free heals still work; pressing the button (or Space) sends `END_TURN`, which the engine resolves as the victory with no discard animation and no enemy playback.
- **Played cards** travel to the scene centre in a fixed layer above the hand bar and stay until the action resolves.
- **Battle effects (AO-D023, DESIGN_LANGUAGE 8):** one `pointer-events: none` layer (`.fx-layer`) over the battlefield, all code-drawn and stepped, all durations scaled by the `--speed` custom property (1 by default). Tile motion (lunge, hit shake, death fade) uses the Web Animations API on `.portrait-frame`; overlays are DOM nodes appended to the layer (`src/ui/fxDom.ts`). Cues come from data, never from re-implemented rules: `cuesFromEvents` (a player action's combat events) and `cuesForStep` (an engine `EnemyStep`) in `battleCues.ts`; `battlePlayback.ts` sequences them.
  - **Attack:** melee lunges 16px toward the target (80ms out, hold, 120ms back) and draws a slash with three sparks on it; ranged units fire a 20x4 bolt, casters and `magic` cards a 8x8 arcane orb with a trail, 240ms in 8 steps; contact = hit shake +-4px (4 steps, 200ms) and a one-frame screen-blend flash. A stack wiped by the blow is snapshotted first and plays the death fade (count to `x0`, collapse and fade in 6 steps, 400ms) over the emptied slot. Multi-target cards fire at every target together (100ms apart on impact).
  - **Block ring** (steel octagon, 3 steps) on Block gain and on any hit that Block absorbed part of; **heal** plus signs rise from the tile, **buff** chevrons rise, **debuff** chevrons and damage-over-time ticks fall; **freeze** shows frost shards around the tile and the ice overlay growing from the centre (4 steps), thaw shatters the shards outward; **chains** close over the tile in 3 steps and break (split, drop 16px, fade) when the flag expires. Thaw and chain break are detected by comparing the displayed board's states, so they also fire when the effect expires at the start of your turn.
  - **Enemy turn:** `END_TURN` still resolves atomically in the engine. The UI takes the result, discards the leftover hand, then replays `enemySteps` (returned by the run action result) on the displayed board (turn chip says Enemy turn): per action a gold rim on the actor (120ms), its lunge or projectile, then the step's `resulting` snapshots and statuses are applied and the floaters show the real numbers, counterattacks get their own beat; about 640ms per action. It then lands on the engine's final state; in dev the replayed board is compared with it and a mismatch is logged (`replayMismatches`). The saved run stays at the pre-turn state until the replay ends, and a battle-ending blow (either side) keeps the field up for a beat before the reward or defeat screen.
  - **Known limits:** no card gives Block today, so the ring shows on absorbed hits.
- **Removed on purpose (AO-D003):** enemy intent text/bubble, threatened pulse, hover highlight of attackers, hover damage preview, HP/block bars and the lock icon (AO-D024).
