# Screen Spec

Current layout and behavior of every screen. Items marked **[PLANNED]** are decided but not built (owned by tasks in `tasks/`). Viewport target 1366x900.

## Shared UI pieces (AO-030)
- **Tooltip (`Tip`, `src/ui/Tip.tsx`):** the only tooltip; no native `title` attribute is used anywhere. `<Tip tip={...}>child</Tip>` shows one `.tip` node in a portal at once (0 ms delay) on pointer hover or keyboard focus (elements that are not natively focusable get `tabIndex=0`), placed 8px from its target below or above (`placeTip`, tested) and always inside the viewport with an 8px margin, following the target, hidden on leave, blur, Esc, scroll, pointer down or when the target disappears. Content is `{title, icon, tag, body, lines[]}` built from data in `src/ui/tipContent.ts` (status effects with their amount and remaining turns, Block, relics with rarity and full text, role badges, Mana cost gem "Costs N Mana to play", Deck/Discard piles, Gold, Food with daily upkeep/production/net and days left, Threat with the enemy size multiplier, Day, Chapter, Boss day, building slots, building cost/effect, hero stats). Status names and effect texts come from the engine (`STATUS_INFO`, `statusEffectText(id, amount)`), never a UI copy. Relic tips show the benefit text in the normal colour and each of the relic's `drawbacks` as its own red line (`relicBenefit` strips the drawbacks from the description); the Mana cost tip of a cheaper "+" card reads "Costs 1 (was 2)."
- **Modal (`src/ui/Modal.tsx`):** the one popup shell (unit, card, deck/pile, hero, food, pause, settings, city buildings, event pickers, city confirm, card removal). Portal, backdrop = the layer (click outside or the close button or Esc closes the top modal only), gold plaque header overlapping the top edge, stone / iron / wood panel, optional gold trim for info popups, focus moves into it, Tab is trapped, focus returns to the opener. A modal opened over another gets a darker backdrop. Omitting `onClose` makes a decision modal (Army full) that cannot be dismissed. While any modal is open Space does not end the turn.
- **Card info popup (`CardInfo.tsx`, AO-D040):** right-click any card (hand, reward, merchant, removal picker, event card picker, deck and pile viewers) calls `useCardInfo().open`. Gold-trim modal titled with the resolved card name ("Charge +" when upgraded, gold name on the card): the large card on the left, on the right type and rarity, source (unit skill / hero card / neutral), full text, Mana cost (with "was N" when the "+" is cheaper), Exhaust / Retain notes, and a well with the play condition (`cardRequirement`, warning icon) and, in battle, the current `cardPlayability` (green "Playable right now." or the red reason).
- **Conditional cards (AO-D040):** a hand card whose condition or Mana is not met is dimmed. When the cause is not just Mana it also carries a small warning icon top-right; hovering shows a tip with the reason (`cardPlayability`) and the requirement, and clicking it toasts the reason instead of doing nothing. Large cards (reward, merchant, pickers) show the requirement as one small red line with a warning icon under the text (tooltip-only was too hidden where the player chooses what to add).
- **Deck viewer (`DeckViewer.tsx`):** read-only modal of hand-size cards in a well, grouped with a `xN` chip, sorted by Mana cost then name (never draw order), upgraded copies listed apart with a `+` name, tabs with counts (`Tab` plates). Right-click a card for its info popup (stacked).

## Card upgrades in the UI (AO-D060, AO-035)
A "+" card whose effective cost is lower than the base card shows its cost gem in moss green (`.cost-cheaper`, on the hand card and on large cards) with the "was N" tip; an unaffordable card keeps the grey gem. `cardView` carries `baseManaCost` for this.
`src/ui/cardView.ts` (`cardView(cardId, upgraded)`, tested) is the one place the UI asks "what does this card show and cost": it reads `resolveCard` (name "Charge +", effective Mana cost) and `CARD_UPGRADES[id].description` for the text. Hand cards, large cards, the card info popup, the deck viewer (grouped and sorted by the effective cost, "+" copies apart) and the card in flight all use it; `handleCardClick`, the hand's affordability/condition marks and `cardPlayability(cardId, state, upgraded)` use the instance's `upgraded` flag, so an upgraded card is played (and refused) at its real cost. A "+" card has a gold name.

## Title
`TitleScreen` (`data-screen="title"`): candle-glow vignette, a code-drawn pixel castle skyline along the bottom (`TitleSkyline`, one SVG of stepped rects in three flat layers, no image files), the crest, the gold ribbon plaque "Ashes of Dominion" (Display 64), the tagline and a stone slab (360 wide) holding **New Run** (primary), **Continue** and **Settings**. Without a saved run Continue is disabled and the line "No saved run yet." sits under it.

## Hero + relic (one screen, AO-D029)
`HeroSetupScreen` replaces the two setup steps. Top: title and the commander name input (Start stays disabled while the name is empty or whitespace, with a hint beside the buttons). Left column: three hero cards (portrait, class, tagline, five stat rows with the shared stat tips) and the five starting relics from `startingRelicList()` as two-column choice cards (64px rarity-framed icon, name, benefit text with each drawback as a red line; hover shows the relic tip). The first hero and relic are preselected; the selected card carries the gold triple line. Right column, sticky (`.hero-setup-side`): the preview from `previewStart(heroId, relicId)` (army in Front / Back rows with unit art, counts and names, then Units, Max Mana, Gold, Food and the relic's `effectSummary` lines), so picking a hero or relic changes it at once (Traveler's Purse: Gold 150, Royal Banner: a larger stack). Under the preview, in the same sticky column so it is visible at 1366x900 and 1280x720 without scrolling, sit **Back** (returns to the title) and one **Start** button that calls `createRun(seed, heroId, name, relicId)` and lands on the Road.

## Road (world map) — meta family
- **Scene (top, fills remaining height):** `Chapter n — Step x / y`, the "You are here — <type>" pill (the `start` node reads "Start"), an always-available **Enter City** button beside it, a red **food alert** strip when `foodWarning` (starving forecast with expected deaths and the morale malus, or days of Food left), "Choose Your Path" and the path cards. When the boss window opens (`bossWarning`, last 7 days) a red banner "The boss draws near: N days left." shows once per chapter per session (click or 6 s to dismiss).
- **Path cards:** 250w x 300h plaques for battle / elite / resource / merchant / event / boss, node-type accent border, corner badge icon. A node shows when its visibility is not `unknown` or its layer is within `worldMap.revealedUntilStep`; layers beyond the next one that were scouted appear as a small "Scouted ahead" icon strip under the cards.
- **Enter City:** opens a confirm popup with `CITY_VISIT_WARNING`, the current enemy strength (`enemyStrengthAfterCityVisits(run)`, x1.00 at Threat 0) and the strength after one more visit; Stay on the Road / Enter City (`TRAVEL_TO_CITY`, no days, no Food). Leaving the city returns to the map and toasts "Enemies grew stronger: Threat N (xM enemy strength)".
- **Bottom bar (200px, shared with City; one component, `GarrisonBar`, takes the run):** 3px top border, padding 8px 16px, 181px content height, 8px spacing scale (4px only between relic cells), column gap 16. Four columns:
  1. Resources (272 content + 16 padding + 4px divider), four 32px rows with 8px gaps; pills use 8px side padding and size by content (`min-width: max-content`) so `Day 99`, five-digit Gold or `123  -2/day` plus the starvation icon never reach the stepped clip: **Gold | Threat**; **Food** (stockpile and daily net, e.g. `50  -2/day`, red with a warning glyph when `foodWarning`; click opens the Food popup with per-stack upkeep, Farm production, net, days left, starvation forecast); **Day | Ch. n/3**; **Boss in N days** (turns red and pulses in 1s pixel steps inside the warning window; static under reduced motion).
  2. Hero (156 content + 16 + 2px divider): plaque 24h, portrait 48h (a button: click opens the **Hero popup**), 3x5 relic grid of 28x28 cells with 4px gaps (hover = relic tip with rarity and full text; empty = dashed).
  3. Army (flexible zone, block centered): the 3x2 grid in true board positions. Top row = front, positions 1-3; bottom row = back, positions 4-6; columns = left / center / right lane. A 16px gutter left of the grid carries the rotated FRONT (accent) and BACK (dim) row labels. Slots are 86h, 240w (shrink to a 168 minimum on narrow viewports), 8px gaps. Filled slot: 64x64 icon with a 20px role badge, count `×N` and unit name; empty slot: dashed "Empty".
  4. Buttons (64 wide): **Deck** (opens the deck viewer: every owned card, tabs All / Attack / Defense / Buff / Debuff / Utility, in the City with a **Remove a card** button that opens the Town Hall where removal lives), Log (history drawer) and Menu, each 64x48.
- **Reposition (AO-D015/D017):** left-click a filled slot to pick the stack up, then click another slot (empty = move, occupied = swap). Right-click a stack opens the unit popup: stats, food use per day (`stackUpkeep`), Split / Merge, and **Dismiss** (count input + two-step confirm; whole stack allowed unless it is the last unit type, then at most count-1) calling `DISMISS_STACK`, outside battle only.
- Toasts (7 s for event outcome texts) and the History drawer use `runEventText.ts`: STARVED ("3 Swordsmen, 1 Archer starved"), BATTLE_LOOT, CHAPTER_STARTED, BOSS_DEFEATED, CITY_VISITED, THREAT_CHANGED, UNITS_GAINED/LOST/DISMISSED, UNIT_GAIN_DECLINED, FARM_UPGRADED, MAGE_TOWER_UPGRADED, DAILY_INCOME, EVENT_RESOLVED text.
- No floating corner menu button, no "New Run" (the pause menu has Main Menu).

## City — meta family
- Scene: sky gradient + CSS castle skyline; hotspots for Town Hall, Barracks, Temple and the eight buildings (built = solid, locked = dashed with cost). Popups per hotspot.
- Town Hall popup: level up, plus **Remove a card**: free the first time, then `cityRemovalPrice` (50, 100, 200 ... Gold, no daily limit; deck floor 5); the popup text shows the price curve and the button shows the current price or the disabled reason (`cardRemovalQuote`).
- Mage Tower popup (built): tier (I-III), `mageTowerDescription`, **Upgrade to Tier N - X Gold** (replaced by "Max tier" at III).
- **Farm** popup: `farmDescription`; built = "Tier N: +F Food per day" and **Upgrade to Tier N+1 - X Gold** (`UPGRADE_FARM`, 5 tiers I-V, "Max tier" at V). The hotspot sub-label shows the tier.
- Barracks popup: four units, count input, cost, single **Recruit** button. No garrison, no Fort.
- Bottom bar: the same `GarrisonBar` as Road; resources column shows **Gold | Slots**, the Food pill (same popup) and the **Leave** button (returns to the map).
- Recruiting shows a "+N" flourish inside the recruited stack's slot. Reposition and the unit popup (with Dismiss) work here too.

## Merchant — overlay family
Top bar (title, gold pill), a shelf of 3 card offers + optional relic offer (rarity frame: common grey, rare steel, epic purple, rarity word on top, price under it), unaffordable = dimmed with a red price, ribbon **Leave** at the bottom-left. Below the shelf a **Remove a card (price)** button opens the deck picker (price rises per use; disabled reasons shown). A toast confirms the removal; the screen stays open.

## Event — overlay family
Rendered from `eventView(run)`: centered `?` badge, plaque title, description, option cards (label + effect text). An option with `available: false` is dimmed and shows its `reason` in red and does not react to clicks. A click calls `CHOOSE_EVENT_OPTION`; the outcome text (`EVENT_RESOLVED.text`) is a long toast and a History line. Sub-steps:
- **Card picker** (`choice.kind = 'card'`): modal grid of the eligible cards, identical copies grouped into one card with an `xN` chip (a click acts on the first copy; the engine already lists only `isUpgradable` instances for an upgrade), scrolling inside the modal so the last row is always reachable, titled "Choose a card to upgrade / remove / give away", close button = `CANCEL_EVENT_CHOICE`, a click = `CHOOSE_EVENT_CARD`. For an upgrade every card is shown as its "+" version with the gold "Upgrade" tag and a "Now: ..." line with the current text.
- **Unit picker** (`choice.kind = 'unit'`, Mercenary Camp): two unit tiles, click = `CHOOSE_EVENT_UNIT`, Cancel = `CANCEL_EVENT_CHOICE`.
- **Army full** (`run.pendingUnitChoice`): modal "Your army is full" with the outcome text and all army stacks plus the newcomer (tagged New, gold ring) as tiles showing count and food per day; a click opens the unit popup where Dismiss (whole or part, the newcomer included) calls `DISMISS_STACK`; **Turn the <unit> away** calls `DECLINE_UNIT_GAIN`. The event closes when the engine settles it.
- An ambush outcome hands the run to the battle screen, then the usual reward.

## Reward — overlay family
Banner "Victory! Choose One" ("The Boss Has Fallen!" after a boss), a loot line (pills "+N Gold", "+N Food" from the last `BATTLE_LOOT`), an optional relic row, up to 3 large cards (cost medallion, icon, name, description), then **Remove a card (free)** and **Skip**.
- **Relic row:** an elite victory shows its `relicOffer` ("Elite spoils: take a relic"), a boss victory shows `relicChoices` ("Boss spoils: take one relic", 3 cards). Each is a relic card (`RelicOfferCard`, also used by the Merchant) with a rarity frame (common grey, rare steel, epic purple), rarity word, benefit text plus a red drawback line, the relic tooltip on hover and a relic info popup on right-click (icon, rarity, text); a click calls `CLAIM_RELIC`, the row disappears and the screen stays open for the normal pick. Leaving forfeits an unclaimed relic (stated on screen).
- **Upgrade slot:** a not-yet-upgraded deck card from `upgradeOptions` appears as the base card previewed as its "+" version (`resolveCard(cardId, true)`: "Charge +", the "+" text and cost) with the gold "Upgrade" tag and a line "Now (N Mana): <base text>"; a click calls `CLAIM_UPGRADE`.
- Card pick: one pick only, no Confirm: clicking a card (or upgrade) applies it and the screen closes.

## Deck picker (shared)
Modal (plaque "Remove a card - price") listing the whole master deck as large cards, identical copies grouped with an `xN` chip; clicking one removes one copy of it and closes the picker, right-click shows its info. Used by Reward, Merchant and City. Modals render in a portal so clipped popups cannot trap them.

## Hero popup (AO-D040)
Click the hero portrait (Road/City bar, battle hero chip). Gold-trim modal titled with the hero name: portrait and class on the left; on the right a well with one row per stat (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Vitality, Wisdom: icon, value, what it does now, derived from the engine formulas) plus Mana (max), the relic list as 48px rarity frames each with its relic tip, and a dashed **Level / XP - Coming soon** block (no XP mechanic exists yet, AO-D030; the hero has `level`/`xp` fields that nothing changes). Intelligence and Vitality say "Not used in combat yet" because the engine does not read them.

## Defeat / Run complete (AO-D027, AO-031)
`RunEndScreen` (`data-screen` defeat or victory), one component: ribbon plaque (blood for Defeat), the cause line (`runSummary(run).causeLabel`: "Your hero fell / Your army was wiped out / Your starving army broke. The run ends here."; Victory says the run is complete), hero name and class, then three wells of `.row`s with pixel icons built from `runSummary(run).rows` (a row the engine does not send is omitted): **Battle** (battles won, elites, bosses, enemies killed, damage dealt/taken, turns, cards played), **Journey** (chapter reached, days, events, Threat, largest stack, units lost / starved / revived), **Economy** (gold and food gathered, food eaten, relics collected). Below: the relics collected (rarity frames with the relic tip) then a row with the final army (unit art, count and name) and the hero stat block (the five stats with their effect tips, plus max Mana). Buttons: **Main Menu** (back to the title, save kept) and **New Run** (clears `aod_run_state_v1`, disables Continue and returns to the title). No floating menu button on this screen.

## Battle — battle family
- **Frame:** full-screen ornate frame (pixel dragon ornaments). Top: raised iron plaques, hero (Display-face name, relics, Mana bar) on the left, turn (turn number, phase, hero HP) on the right. No floating menu button.
- **Body:** left rail = your 2x3 portrait grid (back column, front column), center = scene panel (empty: no narration text, AO-D023; holds the Drop Card zone for no-target cards and the played card of AO-D011; the effects layer draws over the whole field, see Battle effects), right rail = enemy 2x3 grid (front column, back column). **End Turn** is a wide gold bar (`.endturn-bar`) along the bottom of the enemy half of the field (half the field width, right-aligned).
- **Portrait slot (96x112):** iron frame with a 4px team line (steel = yours, blood = enemy), 88x76 sprite window, role badge top-left, `xCount` bottom-right, name row under the window. No HP strip, no block strip, no lock icon (AO-D024). Block and statuses are 16px icons with their amount stacked under the role badge. Selected = white ring + gold corner pixels; selectable = gold ring; dimmed when not relevant. State visuals (`stackStates` in `stackStatus.ts`, single UI mirror of the engine gate): **acted** (your stack, already used its action) = whole tile faded, not selectable; **frozen** = ice tint, jagged frost rim and shards, not selectable; **chained** (`cannotAttack` or `cannotMove`) = two crossing iron chains over the window, only `cannotAttack` makes it unselectable (a rooted stack can still attack); **blocked** (back-row melee with a living friendly directly in front, AO-D033) = small red no-entry `ui_blocked` marker top-right, not selectable, no overlay. Floating text shows `-N units` for kills and `Wounded` when the hit killed nothing; the battle log line carries HP damage, units killed and units left.
- **Bottom bar:** Deck pile (count) at far left, hand in a flat row (no fan), Discard pile (count) at the right of the hand, then a vertical column of two rectangular buttons at the far right: Log, Settings. The draw pile is drawn as a stack. The piles are buttons: clicking either opens the pile viewer (deck viewer with tabs Draw / Discard, and Exhausted when it has cards; headers "Draw pile - N", "Discard pile - N"; contents grouped and sorted by cost with the order hidden). The hero chip portrait opens the Hero popup. Draw = cards slide in from the deck; end of turn = leftover cards slide to the discard pile.
- **Interaction:** click your stack to start its free action, then click a legal enemy (Priest: any friendly stack). Card flow: click card, then the required targets (or the Drop Card zone). Re-click a selected stack to deselect; `Esc` cancels; a click on bare battlefield (not a unit, the Drop Card zone or End Turn) deselects the stack and cancels the pending card; `Space` ends the turn when nothing is pending, no popup or the log is open and you are not typing. While an effect sequence runs (a played action, the enemy turn) a click anywhere or `Space` skips to its end and lands on the engine's final state; input is locked until then. Dead enemy stacks never act in playback.
- **No pos badge, no HP numbers** on portraits; the count is the health readout. Block and statuses appear as small icons with their amount on the portrait image (top-left), not as text under the name.
- **Right-click** any stack (yours or enemy) opens the unit info popup without split/merge, plus morale, veterancy, block and active statuses (each with its effect tip). Right-click a hand card opens the card info popup with the live playability reason.
- **Floating combat text** rises over the affected portrait for your actions and the enemy's alike: `-N units` / `Wounded` (AO-D022), `Dodged`, `Blocked N`, `+N Block`, heal `+N units` (the soldiers actually restored), and a status name with its icon. Reduced motion keeps the floaters and drops every effect.
- **Cannot act** (acted, frozen, cannotAttack, blocked back-row melee): the state visuals above, not selectable; no warning text.
- **Played cards** travel to the scene centre in a fixed layer above the hand bar and stay until the action resolves.
- **Battle effects (AO-D023, DESIGN_LANGUAGE 8):** one `pointer-events: none` layer (`.fx-layer`) over the battlefield, all code-drawn and stepped, all durations scaled by the `--speed` custom property (1 by default). Tile motion (lunge, hit shake, death fade) uses the Web Animations API on `.portrait-frame`; overlays are DOM nodes appended to the layer (`src/ui/fxDom.ts`). Cues come from data, never from re-implemented rules: `cuesFromEvents` (a player action's combat events) and `cuesForStep` (an engine `EnemyStep`) in `battleCues.ts`; `battlePlayback.ts` sequences them.
  - **Attack:** melee lunges 16px toward the target (80ms out, hold, 120ms back) and draws a slash with three sparks on it; ranged units fire a 20x4 bolt, casters and `magic` cards a 8x8 arcane orb with a trail, 240ms in 8 steps; contact = hit shake +-4px (4 steps, 200ms) and a one-frame screen-blend flash. A stack wiped by the blow is snapshotted first and plays the death fade (count to `x0`, collapse and fade in 6 steps, 400ms) over the emptied slot. Multi-target cards fire at every target together (100ms apart on impact).
  - **Block ring** (steel octagon, 3 steps) on Block gain and on any hit that Block absorbed part of; **heal** plus signs rise from the tile, **buff** chevrons rise, **debuff** chevrons and damage-over-time ticks fall; **freeze** shows frost shards around the tile and the ice overlay growing from the centre (4 steps), thaw shatters the shards outward; **chains** close over the tile in 3 steps and break (split, drop 16px, fade) when the flag expires. Thaw and chain break are detected by comparing the displayed board's states, so they also fire when the effect expires at the start of your turn.
  - **Enemy turn:** `END_TURN` still resolves atomically in the engine. The UI takes the result, discards the leftover hand, then replays `enemySteps` (returned by the run action result) on the displayed board (turn chip says Enemy turn): per action a gold rim on the actor (120ms), its lunge or projectile, then the step's `resulting` snapshots and statuses are applied and the floaters show the real numbers, counterattacks get their own beat; about 640ms per action. It then lands on the engine's final state; in dev the replayed board is compared with it and a mismatch is logged (`replayMismatches`). The saved run stays at the pre-turn state until the replay ends, and a battle-ending blow (either side) keeps the field up for a beat before the reward or defeat screen.
  - **Known limits:** no card gives Block today, so the ring shows on absorbed hits.
- **Removed on purpose (AO-D003):** enemy intent text/bubble, threatened pulse, hover highlight of attackers, hover damage preview, HP/block bars and the lock icon (AO-D024).

## Pause menu / Settings / History drawer
Pause menu: resume, settings (music volume), main menu. History drawer: newest-first numbered log, opened only via a Log button, closed by default.
