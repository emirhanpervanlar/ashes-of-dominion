# Decisions Log

Append-only. Newest last. Format: `AO-D<nn> | date | decision | why`.

- AO-D001 | 2026-09 | Combat is Mana-only; AP/DP and Energy are excluded from MVP. | v3 canonical.
- AO-D002 | 2026-09 | Melee units may only target enemy FRONT row in their lane geometry. No fallback to the backline when a front slot is empty. Only ranged (`rangedAllAccess`) units reach the backline. | Owner: front units must not hit back units.
- AO-D003 | 2026-09 | Enemy intent / "who attacks whom" previews are removed (text, bubble, threatened pulse, hover highlight, hover damage preview). The end-of-turn playback may still glow the acting stack and shake the hit stack. | Owner: looked bad.
- AO-D004 | 2026-09 | Stack health is total HP = count x hpPerUnit; losing HP reduces count (Heroes 3 model). Heals restore HP, each `hpPerUnit` of heal = 1 unit, capped at the stack's pre-battle count (`preBattleMaxCount`). Battle UI does NOT display raw HP numbers. | Owner.
- AO-D005 | 2026-09 | "1 turn" self-lockdown flags (`cannotAttack`, `cannotMove`, `incomingDamageReductionPercent`) expire at the start of the owner's next turn. Protect's redirect is consumed by the next hit it redirects. | Bug: flags previously stuck forever.
- AO-D006 | 2026-09 | Post-battle rewards: no relics; at most 3 choices total (new cards + upgrades combined), Slay-the-Spire style screen. | Owner.
- AO-D007 | 2026-09 | Starting armies are small: Warlord Swordsman6+Knight2, Rogue Archer6+Knight2, Mage Archer4+Priest4. | Owner: too easy.
- AO-D008 | 2026-09 | City garrison mechanic removed; recruits join the field army directly (max 6 stacks). | Owner.
- AO-D009 | 2026-09 | Hero HP is not shown on the Road/City bars (mechanic considered meaningless). Engine field kept. | Owner.
- AO-D010 | 2026-09 | Road and City share one bottom bar (200px): resources | hero plaque+portrait+3x5 relic grid | 6 unit slots (empty = dashed "Empty") | Log + Menu fixed-size buttons. Road unit slot width 130px. | Owner.
- AO-D011 | 2026-09 | Battle UI: no pos badge, no HP numbers, buffs/debuffs as icon plus amount on the portrait, right-click opens unit info, floating combat text for the players own actions, stacks that cannot act show a lock icon and no hover, cannot-attack warning text removed, played cards travel to scene centre and stay visible. Road path cards 250x300. | Owner (planned, tasks AO-002/AO-003).
- AO-D012 | 2026-09 | Working model: owner decides; Claude Director turns intent into tasks and dispatches specialists (gameplay-combat, gameplay-economy, ui-ux, ui-frontend, qa-automated, qa-playtest, reviewer). Agents never change design; they raise a DDR. No GitHub Actions (API-key billing); everything runs locally in-session. | Owner.
- AO-D013 | 2026-09 | Amends AO-D002: melee (non-ranged) units may target the enemy backline ONLY when the enemy front row (positions 1-3) has no living stack; while any front stack lives, melee still reaches front stacks only. Same rule for enemy AI against the player. | Owner: fixes softlock (goblin left at position 5 vs melee army) and the untouchable all-backline Mage army.
- AO-D014 | 2026-09 | Royal Banner grants +6 to the largest starting stack (was +20). | Owner: +20 undid the small-army decision AO-D007.
- AO-D015 | 2026-09 | Recruiting merges into an existing stack of the same unit type, otherwise takes the first free slot. Outside battle (Road, City) the player can reposition their stacks at any time; in battle positions change only through cards. | Owner.
- AO-D016 | 2026-09 | Road and City bottom bar shows the army as a 3-column x 2-row grid matching real board positions (top row = front 1-3, bottom row = back 4-6, columns = left/center/right lane) instead of 6 slots in a line; remaining bar columns are re-proportioned so the whole bar stays cohesive (no drift, no arbitrary sizes). | Owner.
- AO-D017 | 2026-09 | Road/City army grid: left-click a filled slot picks the stack up (click another slot to swap/move it; Esc cancels); right-click a stack opens the info/split/merge popup (same convention as battle, AO-D011). Dropping onto a same-type stack swaps, never merges; merging stays in the popup. Army slots are 240x86 (icon left, count + name right) so two rows fit in the 200px bar; this supersedes the 130px square slots of AO-D010. Director call, reversible on request. | Derived from AO-D015/D016 via tasks/AO-008-bar-spec.md.
- AO-D018 | 2026-09 | Damage follows Heroes 3: per-unit damage is scaled by an attack-vs-defense percentage modifier (attack > defense: +5% per point, max +300%; defense > attack: -2.5% per point, max -70%), so a hit never deals 0. Damage is linear in unit count (the count-tier multipliers 0.9/0.75/... are removed). Supersedes the `max(0, attack - defense)` model. | Owner: "stick to what Heroes 3 does"; also removes the split-stack exploit.
- AO-D019 | 2026-09 | After a battle surviving units are fully healed (Heroes 3); only dead units are permanently lost. Shrine is redesigned as revival (see AO-D020). | Owner.
- AO-D020 | 2026-09 | City building effects: Stable = movement Food cost -25%; Forge = army attack +5%; Mage Tower = hero Wisdom +2 (Mana / heal) - if no Wisdom stat exists yet, raise a DDR; Shrine = after every battle 10% of casualties revive; Gold Mine = +10 Gold per day (replaces the one-off +100). Market, Training Hall unchanged. | Owner accepted Director proposal.
- AO-D021 | 2026-09 | Melee reach also has a lane rule: a melee unit may target only enemies in its own lane or an adjacent lane (left-center, center-right; left never reaches right). AO-D013 (backline only when the front row is empty) still applies on top. Ranged units are unrestricted. | Owner bug report: left front unit hit the right back unit.
- AO-D022 | 2026-09 | Damage shown to the player is expressed in units killed (floating text "-2 units", "Wounded" when nothing died), the combat log records both HP damage and units killed. | Owner.
- AO-D023 | 2026-09 | Enemy turns are played back step by step with real numbers: the engine returns each enemy action as an ordered step list. Battle visuals are code-drawn effects (lunge, slash, projectile, block ring, buff/heal particles, freeze ice, hit shake, death fade) on the battlefield; no centre narration text; no art assets for now. | Owner.
- AO-D024 | 2026-09 | A unit that already acted this turn is shown faded (no lock icon). Frozen = ice overlay; "cannot move/act" effects = chains. Battle HP bars are removed; clicking empty space deselects the selection. End Turn becomes a wide bar at the bottom of the enemy area. | Owner.
- AO-D025 | 2026-09 | One shared instant tooltip component (relics, reward card mana cost, role badges, buffs/debuffs with their real effect text from data, resources, buildings, Deck/Discard). | Owner.
- AO-D026 | 2026-09 | Reward screen: no Confirm button, a click applies immediately; one pick only (card, upgrade or skip). Card REMOVAL (thin the deck) is added to Reward, Merchant and City (City: once per week); prices/limits to be set by a DDR. | Owner.
- AO-D027 | 2026-09 | Defeat screen shows full run statistics (enemies killed, resources gathered, turns, largest stack, damage dealt/taken, etc.); New Run returns to the main menu and clears the saved run. Card/hero unlocks after death are a planned feature (needs a persistent profile), not built now. | Owner.
- AO-D028 | 2026-09 | Starting deck is simplified: mostly attack/block with at most 1-2 simple buffs, no debuffs; complex cards move to rewards/merchant. Exact list via DDR. | Owner.
- AO-D029 | 2026-09 | Hero and starting relic are chosen on ONE screen; some relics carry a negative drawback next to the benefit. Exact relic list via DDR. | Owner.
- AO-D030 | 2026-09 | Hero XP / levels / stats and later unit XP: planned, to be designed in a dedicated owner discussion before any implementation. | Owner.
- AO-D031 | 2026-09 | Damage model B (Heroes 3 proper): every unit gets a separate base `damage` stat; attack and defense are only the percentage modifier of AO-D018 (attack > defense +5%/pt max +300%, defense > attack -2.5%/pt max -70%). Base damage values are chosen so typical matchups stay near the old magnitudes, then balanced by qa-playtest simulation. Supersedes the "attack is the base damage" reading of AO-D018. Later (far future, not now): elemental/damage types (poison, fire, freeze, lightning, ...) and immunities. | Owner: stay faithful to H3, extend with mechanics later.
- AO-D032 | 2026-09 | Taunt stays but never overrides reach: a back-row Taunt cannot be hit by a front-row melee unit, and a back-row melee unit cannot hit a front-row Taunt. Melee lane rule (AO-D021) and front/back rule (AO-D013) apply to Taunt targets too. | Owner.
- AO-D033 | 2026-09 | Disciples rule: a melee unit in the BACK row that has a living friendly unit directly in front of it (same lane, front row) cannot attack at all. If the front slot of its lane is empty it can attack per AO-D013/D021. Ranged units are unaffected. Applies to player and enemy. | Owner. Interpretation "same lane" chosen by the Director; reversible.
- AO-D034 | 2026-09 | Healing is linear in unit count like damage (no count-tier scaling). | Owner.
- AO-D035 | 2026-09 | Card removal: Reward free (uses the single pick), Merchant 50 Gold +25 per use, City free once per 7 days, deck never below 5 cards. Constants in run/cardRemoval.ts. | Owner accepted proposal A.
- AO-D036 | 2026-09 | Mage Tower grants max Mana (not Wisdom) in three upgradeable tiers with steeply rising cost and cumulative bonus +1 / +3 / +6 (costs 80 / 200 / 500 Gold): cheap early, hard to reach late, very rewarding because more Mana = more cards per turn. Numbers are a Director proposal, reversible on request. | Owner: "small early values, compounding later, satisfying when reached".
- AO-D037 | 2026-09 | Relics: the starting relics are a separate pool of exactly 5 simple relics, all offered on the hero-choice screen; not every relic needs a drawback, some non-starting relics carry one. Relics have a rarity (common / rare / epic). Sources: elite battles and events (rarely), merchants (price rises with rarity/power). Relics are NOT rewarded after normal battles (AO-D006 stays). Planned later: new starting relics are unlocked after runs based on the player's achievements (needs the persistent profile, see AO-D027) - not built now. | Owner.
- AO-D038 | 2026-09 | Amends AO-D021 fallback: the lane-rule softlock guard applies only to a true stalemate (no unit on either side can reach any target under the lane/front-back rules and no ranged unit exists), not per attacker. Otherwise a unit that cannot reach anything simply does not attack (owner bug: enemy right-lane unit hit my last left-lane unit). | Owner bug report.
- AO-D039 | 2026-09 | The first 3 steps of the road never contain an Elite Battle. | Owner: unwinnable early.
- AO-D040 | 2026-09 | Cards whose use depends on a condition (e.g. Charge needs a Knight) must explain it: a warning/tooltip on the card and a clear reason when unplayable; the unit info popup convention is extended to cards (right-click a card anywhere -> card info popup). Battle draw pile and discard pile each get a viewer (contents shown sorted, not in draw order); outside battle a Deck viewer opens from a button in the Road/City bottom bar; clicking the hero portrait opens a Hero popup with stats (XP/level section arrives with the hero design discussion, AO-D030). | Owner.
- AO-D041 | 2026-09 | The visual language in docs/DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md (medieval pixel art, HoMM3/Disciples feel, muted palette, no emoji, no border-radius, palette-indexed pixel icons, MedievalSharp + Silkscreen + Alegreya Sans) is adopted for ALL screens; the style proof docs/style-proof/index.html is the visual reference.
