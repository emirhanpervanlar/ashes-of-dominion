- AO-D057 | 2026-09 | Starvation (replaces the mild AGENT.md starvation): every day the stockpile cannot cover the upkeep, the army is fully fed with what exists (Food goes to 0) and the shortage costs units: a random share of the whole army dies, the share scales with the shortage relative to the daily need (missing 80 of 100 hurts far more than missing 10 of 100), starts at about 10% on the first fully starved day and RISES with each consecutive starving day. Deaths are random units across stacks (not "cut down until upkeep fits"), and every death is announced ("N <units> starved"). After a set number of consecutive starving days the army also enters battles with negative morale, and the malus grows slightly with every further starving day. The point: the player must not build huge stacks and gamble on food. Balance is critical and will be tuned by tests and play; constants in one block. | Owner.
